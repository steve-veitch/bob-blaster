# Random Events (Code Truth)

## Interval
- Event interval: 30 seconds

## Event definitions
The following events exist with fixed name/duration/effect strings (see schemas/events.schema.json):
- **meteorShower**: Meteor Shower (10s) — Meteors fall from sky dealing damage
- **powerSurge**: Power Surge (15s) — All weapons deal double damage
- **monsterFrenzy**: Monster Frenzy (20s) — Monsters spawn at 2x rate
- **healingRain**: Healing Rain (12s) — Player slowly regenerates health
- **timeWarp**: Time Warp (10s) — Everything moves in slow motion
- **lootBonanza**: Loot Bonanza (15s) — Power-ups spawn at 3x rate
- **fogOfWar**: Fog of War (20s) — Visibility reduced dramatically
- **berserk**: Berserk Mode (10s) — Movement speed doubled, take more damage
