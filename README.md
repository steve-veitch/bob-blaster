## 🎮 How to Play

### Getting Started
1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. Click **"🪙 Insert 1 x Bob Coin to Begin 🪙"**
3. Click to lock your pointer and enter the arena
4. Survive the waves and become a legend!

### Controls

**Movement**
- `W` - Move Forward
- `S` - Move Backward  
- `A` - Move Left
- `D` - Move Right
- `Mouse` - Look Around

**Combat**
- `Space` or `Left Click` - Shoot
- `1` - Bob Blaster (Standard)
- `2` - Problem Spreader (Shotgun)
- `3` - Solution Rifle (Sniper)
- `4` - Rapid Resolver (Machine Gun)
- `5` - Crisis Launcher (Rocket)

**System**
- `ESC` - Pause Game
- Click weapon slots in HUD to switch weapons

### Pro Tips 💡

1. **Weapon Management** - Each weapon excels in different situations:
   - Use the Shotgun for close-range swarms
   - Sniper for distant tanks and bosses
   - Machine Gun for sustained crowd control
   - Rocket for emergency area damage
   - Bob Blaster when you're out of ammo

2. **Power-Up Strategy** - Collect power-ups strategically:
   - Save shields for boss fights
   - Combine rapid fire + damage boost for maximum DPS
   - Use speed boost to collect distant power-ups

3. **Movement is Life** - Keep moving! Standing still is death
   - Circle strafe around enemies
   - Use terrain for cover
   - Position yourself near power-up spawns

4. **Boss Tactics** - Bosses have 3 phases:
   - Phase 1: Learn attack patterns
   - Phase 2: Increase aggression
   - Phase 3: Go all-out with your best weapons

5. **Ammo Conservation** - Bob Blaster has infinite ammo:
   - Use special weapons for tough enemies
   - Fall back to Bob Blaster for basic problems
   - Reload between waves when possible
